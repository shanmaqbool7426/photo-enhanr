Build a FULL AI-powered mobile app called **“Enhance Photo Quality”** using **React Native + Expo** for frontend and **Node.js + Express + MongoDB** for backend.

The app should allow users to upload blurry, low-quality, old, dark, pixelated, or damaged photos and enhance them using AI.

The app must look modern, premium, fast, and smooth like a real production app.

---

* React Navigation

* Expo Image Picker
* Expo Media Library

## Backend
